# INTLY

INTLY is a shared workspace for a small team (2–6 people) to find vacancies, freelance projects and tenders, assess fit, prepare responses and follow up.

## Product contracts

- Shared imports and a common opportunity catalogue; personal profiles, saved searches, responses and preferences.
- Two roles: Admin and User. Administrative operations remain available only to administrators.
- Existing source integrations, permissions, data contracts and workflow behaviour remain intact during the redesign.
- AI can read all relevant project data. An AI action must still clearly state its purpose and show progress, failure and completion.
- The interface supports Russian and English, four palettes, light/dark/system modes, comfortable/compact density and reduced motion.
- Preserve existing animations, keyboard navigation, focus management and responsive behaviour.

## UI and content direction

The owner-provided DESIGN.md is the visual authority for the 2026-10-10 redesign. Adapt its Linear-inspired neutral surfaces, Inter typography, thin borders and restrained accents to the existing application.

Use plain product language: one term per concept, concrete action labels, concise instructions and errors that explain the next step. Keep technical settings where they are needed to configure integrations; keep internal identifiers and implementation details out of ordinary task flows.
